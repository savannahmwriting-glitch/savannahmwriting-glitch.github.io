Poem titled "Some Rendition of the American Dream" was a finalist for the 2025 Spring Writing Contest for Polyphony Lit. This year, Massey's poem was named a runner-up for the Claudia Anne Seaman Poetry Awards, which review all poetry at Polyphony.
