**Overview**
"The Queer South" was a literary portfolio consisting of poetry, creative non-fiction, and fiction for the 2026 Davidson Fellows Scholarship.

in a different time, my mother knows death

early morning. old cars. wishing you well.

It is 3 AM and I Want You Wholly

Mariner of January

silvercreek, mississippi

Eden, Mississippi

Some Rendition of the American Dream

i love you the most when we are drowning

I think I am looking for a nonexistent love

rotting through a hometown

astigmatisms and other sight problems

what we are

the trees have eyes and aunt fae knows it

Dichotomy of Having a Body

list of things, in order, as they kill you

delta boys of midnight, mississippi

[closing scene]

we walk the highway restless and choke

mississippi mud is a call to better places

or artificial sweeteners or addiction or whatever they call it

the reason my father takes me to church
